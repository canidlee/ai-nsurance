Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

AI-nsurance ("we," "us") helps you understand your auto and home insurance. Because you trust us with sensitive documents, we keep this policy short, plain, and honest — the same way we explain insurance.

What we collect

  • Contact information you provide: name and email address.
  • Policy documents you upload for review (declarations pages and related documents), and the answers you give in our forms and quizzes.
  • Payment information is collected and processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, collected through standard analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To analyze the documents you upload and deliver your report. Document analysis is performed using Anthropic's AI services; documents are transmitted securely for that purpose.
  • To send you your results and, if you opt in, educational emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To operate and improve the service.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never share your information with insurance carriers, agents, or lead-generation companies. We don't earn commissions or referral fees from anyone — that independence is the entire point of this service.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the service: Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), Mailchimp (email), Google Workspace (business email), and Anthropic (document analysis). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Retention & deletion

Uploaded policy documents are used to generate your report and are not retained as a standing database. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing help@ai-nsurance.com, and we will honor it promptly.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email help@ai-nsurance.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we deliberately minimize what we collect and keep.

Children

Our service is for adults managing their own insurance and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Email help@ai-nsurance.com.
