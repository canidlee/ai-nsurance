Our Giving Commitment

We're fighting for Milo.

1% of every AI-nsurance subscription goes directly to Miracles for Mighty Milo — a campaign to find a life-saving treatment for Milo, a 7-year-old boy diagnosed with SPAX5 (Spastic Ataxia Type 5), an extraordinarily rare and progressive neurodegenerative disease.

SPAX5 affects only a handful of children in the world. Milo's family — people we know personally — are racing to fund drug repurposing research, stem cell treatments, and gene therapy exploration before the disease progresses further.

When you understand your insurance better, you help a family find a miracle for their son. That connection matters to us.
