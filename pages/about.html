Our Giving Commitment

Clarity that funds a cure.

We pledge 1% of every subscription to a family foundation supporting research into rare mitochondrial diseases — conditions so rare they affect only a handful of people in the world.

The foundation was established by one of our founding partners after a family member was diagnosed. It operates under an established 501(c)(3) umbrella focused on rare genetic diseases.

When you protect your family with AI-nsurance, you're also helping protect another family too.
