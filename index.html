The Costly Mistakes People Make

Most families find at least one thing
worth fixing — and that's good news

Not because anyone misled you — life changes, costs rise, and nobody's looked at the policy since the day you signed. The gap between what you assume and what the page says is fixable, once you can see it.

Coverage Gap

The Flood Assumption

Standard homeowners policies don't cover floods. Many homeowners discover this only after a claim is denied.

Underinsurance

Outdated Replacement Costs

Home values and construction costs have risen dramatically. Most people haven't updated limits in years.

Liability Gap

No Umbrella Coverage

Auto and home liability limits are often far too low to protect assets in a serious accident or lawsuit.

Duplicate Spend

Overlapping Policies

Paying for roadside assistance in multiple places, or rental coverage you already have through a credit card.

Missed Savings

Unclaimed Discounts

Bundling, loyalty, security system, and professional discounts that were never applied to your policy.

Hidden Exclusion

Home Business Exposure

Working from home doesn't mean your business equipment or liability is covered under your homeowners policy.
