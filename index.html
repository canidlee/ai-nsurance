I was paying a reasonable rate through a highly-rated national carrier — great coverage, fast claims, no surprises. Then a friend of a friend who happened to be an insurance broker offered to help me save money. He ran the numbers. A competing carrier came in lower. I switched.

Our story

Why we built this

I was paying a reasonable rate through a highly-rated national carrier. Great coverage. Fast claims. Zero surprises. I didn't think much about it — which, in hindsight, is exactly what a good insurer earns.

Then a friend of a friend, who happened to be an insurance broker, offered to help me save money. Friendly, confident. He ran the numbers. A competing carrier came in lower. I switched.

"I didn't notice the six-month billing cycle. I didn't track the rate adjustments when I leased a new car. By the time I actually did the math, I was paying significantly more than before."

And here's what made it worse: the carrier I'd left consistently ranks at the top of every independent claims satisfaction study. The one I switched to doesn't. I had traded a genuinely excellent insurer for a mediocre one — and I was paying more for the privilege.

What I really wanted was simple: someone to tell me what was best for me, not what was best for them. No website did that. No tool showed me the discounts I was already entitled to. Every site I found wanted to sell me something new.

I spent years thinking about what that tool should look like. This is it.
